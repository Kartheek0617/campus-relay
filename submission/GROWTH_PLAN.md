## 1. Executive Summary

Acquiring 500 engineering students on a ₹2,000 budget within 7 days is impossible through traditional paid advertising (typical EdTech B2C CPA in India ranges between ₹60–₹180 per registration). 

**The Strategy:** We replace paid acquisition with a **Campus Relay Engine** that activates three high-trust peer nodes:
1. **College Technical Clubs & Student Coordinators** (IEEE, GDSC, Coding Clubs).
2. **Campus Captains & Peer Ambassadors** incentivized with learning milestones.
3. **Hyper-targeted student placement circles** and opted-in engineering groups.

A nominal ₹800 (40% of budget) is reserved for a controlled messaging validation experiment, while 100% of organic acquisition is tracked with unique referral codes, anti-abuse deduplication, and single first-touch attribution.

## 2. Target Student Segment & Behavioral Profile

- **Primary Persona:** Final-year engineering students (Class of 2025/2026 across CSE, ECE, IT, AI/DS, ME, EEE).
- **Core Motivations:**
  - **Campus Placement Differentiation:** Need a non-trivial, live project on GitHub and resume to discuss during technical interviews.
  - **Overcoming AI Hype Paralysis:** Want an end-to-end, hands-on build workflow rather than endless theoretical tutorials.
  - **Low Commitment & High Trust:** A focused 60-minute session with zero financial risk (₹0).
- **Registration Barriers & Campaign Countermeasures:**
  | Student Barrier | Psychological Hesitation | Campaign Countermeasure |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Skepticism of Generic Webinars** | "Will this just be a 50-minute sales pitch?" | Transparent curriculum breakdown showing minute-by-minute coding milestones (00–60m). |
  | **Time Scarcity** | "Final year semester exams and project submissions are overwhelming." | Fixed 60-minute duration with no homework or mandatory prerequisite installs. |
  | **Spam Fatigue** | "Broadcast WhatsApp groups are flooded with webinar spam." | Peer ambassadors send personalized, editable 3-variant messages (Portfolio, Placement, Study Group). |
  | **Unsubstantiated Guarantees** | "I don't trust platforms claiming 'Guaranteed 12 LPA Placements'." | Explicit honesty: Zero false job promises; focus on demonstrable engineering competence. |

## 4. Prioritized Acquisition Channels

### Channel 1 — College Clubs & Student Coordinators (Primary Volume: 48%)
- **Mechanism:** Direct outreach to Presidents and Technical Leads of IEEE Student Branches, GDSC chapters, and college coding societies across 20 identified engineering campuses (Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra).
- **Asset:** Official department circular and permission-request template.
- **Conversion Expectation:** 20 clubs × 12 registrations = **240 students**.

### Channel 2 — Campus Ambassadors & Peer Referrals (Viral Multiplication: 32%)
- **Mechanism:** Activating 20 selected student ambassadors (Captains and Peer advocates) with unique tracking codes (`RELAY-XXXX`).
- **Incentive:** Learning milestone unlocks (1 referral = *AI Starter Blueprint*; 10 referrals = *VIP Q&A Placement Pass*; 25 referrals = *Campus Top Contributor*).
- **Conversion Expectation:** 20 ambassadors × 8 referrals = **160 students**.

### Channel 3 — Direct Placement Communities & Circles (Organic Reach: 20%)
- **Mechanism:** Permission-based sharing in moderated college Discord communities, Telegram placement channels, and batchmate project cohorts.
- **Conversion Expectation:** **100 students**.

## 8. Attribution Guardrails & Risk Mitigations

1. **Email Deduplication:** Case-insensitive email hashing rejects repeat submissions.
2. **Self-Referral Prevention:** Users cannot refer their own session code.
3. **First-Touch Referral Lock:** If a student receives multiple links, the first verified code retains credit.
4. **Graceful Fallbacks:** If a typo occurs in a referral code, the registration still succeeds with an unassigned tag rather than dropping the student.
5. **No Forced Virality:** Referrals are encouraged through milestone value, never by paywalling registration.
